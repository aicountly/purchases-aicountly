# Purchases - Aicountly — Google Play listing

## App name

Purchases - Aicountly

## Short description (80)

Approve purchase orders and count deliveries at the gate by scanning barcodes.

## Full description

Purchases is the purchasing desk for your company, on your phone.

Approve: purchase orders, order changes and requisitions waiting for your decision, with their lines, value and the reason they need you. Approve, or reject with a reason that stays on the record.

Receive: open an expected delivery and scan item barcodes with the camera — each scan counts one unit on its order line. Record shortages, reject damaged goods with a reason, and capture batch, expiry and serial numbers for items that track them. If the signal drops at the gate, keep counting: the count is kept on the phone until you record it, and a delivery is never recorded twice.

Track: find a purchase order by number or supplier and see whether it is approved, issued, received and billed. Look up a supplier's open orders and what your books say you owe them.

Purchases opens the companies your administrator has shared with you in AICOUNTLY. Writing purchase orders, bills, returns and reports stay on the web.

## Data safety (summary)

- Collected: name, email address, user ID (account management, app functionality); purchase orders and receipts you record (app functionality). Not shared with third parties. Not used for advertising.
- Camera: barcode reading only; no images are stored or sent.
- Encrypted in transit. Account deletion: in the app (Account → Delete my AICOUNTLY account) and at https://aicountly.com/delete-account.
